# Login

## Purpose

Use the Login page to sign in to the HRMS with your work email and password. After signing in, you are taken to the dashboard (or the Employee Self-Service area if you have an employee role).

## Screenshot

![Login page](/manual/pages/login.png)

## On this page

1. **Logo** — Application logo at the top of the card.
2. **Email** — Field to enter your email address.
3. **Password** — Field to enter your password.
4. **Show/hide password** — Button to toggle between hiding and showing the password.
5. **Reset Password** — Link to open the reset-password flow if you forgot your password.
6. **Sign in** — Button to submit the form and sign in.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Logo | Shows the application branding. | Display only. |
| 2 | Email | Enter your email address. | Must be a valid email format. |
| 3 | Password | Enter your password. | Must be at least 6 characters. |
| 4 | Show/hide password | Toggles password visibility. | Use to check what you typed. |
| 5 | Reset Password | Opens the reset-password page. | Use if you forgot your password. |
| 6 | Sign in | Submits the form and signs you in. | Disabled while signing in; shows "Signing in...". |

## Common tasks

- **Sign in** — Enter your email and password, then click **Sign in**. You are taken to the dashboard or ESS depending on your role.
- **Open reset password** — Click **Reset Password** to request a password reset link by email.
- **Toggle password visibility** — Click the eye icon next to the password field to show or hide the password.
- **Change password on first login** — If this is your first login, you will be asked to change your password before continuing.

## Troubleshooting

- **Invalid email** — Make sure you enter a valid email address (e.g. `name@company.com`). The field will show an error if the format is wrong.
- **Wrong password** — If the password is incorrect, a message appears. Check caps lock and try again, or use **Reset Password**.
- **"Signing in..." never finishes** — Check your internet connection. If it persists, try again later or contact your administrator.
- **Page does not load** — Ensure you are using the correct URL and that the HRMS is available. Contact IT if the problem continues.
- **Reset Password link not working** — Ensure pop-ups are not blocked and you are using a supported browser. Try the link again or contact your admin.
