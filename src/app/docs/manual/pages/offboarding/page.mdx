# Employee Offboarding Records

> **Flagged:** This section is draft documentation. The offboarding feature is not fully fixed yet. Screenshots and steps will be updated once the feature is complete.

## Purpose

View and manage offboarding records. You can search and filter the list, start a new offboarding process, or open a record to see its detail or adjust salary.

## Screenshot

![Employee Offboarding Records](/manual/pages/offboarding.png)

## On this page

1. **Search by Employee Name or ID** — Type to find offboarding records.
2. **Position filter** — Narrow the list by position.
3. **Department filter** — Narrow the list by department.
4. **Advanced Search** — Open more filter options.
5. **New Offboarding Process** — Start a new offboarding process.
6. **Offboarding table** — Rows show Name, Position, Department, Join Date, Status.
7. **Preview link** — Open the offboarding detail for that record (URL includes the record ID).
8. **Pagination** — Move between pages of results.

## Common tasks

- **View offboarding list** — Use search and filters, then click **Preview** on a row to open its detail.
- **Start offboarding** — Click **New Offboarding Process** and follow the steps.
- **Open detail** — Click **Preview** on a row. The detail URL contains the record ID (e.g. .../off-boarding/16).

## Troubleshooting

- **List is empty** — Check filters and search; clear them if needed. If you don't have access, ask your admin.
- **Cannot start new process** — This may be restricted by role. If you don't see it, ask your admin.
