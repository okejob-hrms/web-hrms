# Reset Password

## Purpose

Use this page to request a password reset link. Enter your registered email and the system sends you a link to set a new password.

## Screenshot

![Reset Password](/manual/pages/reset-password.png)

## On this page

1. **Back** — Link to return to the previous page (e.g. login).
2. **Email** — Field to enter your registered email address.
3. **Continue** — Button to submit and send the reset link.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Back | Returns to the previous page. | Use to go back to login. |
| 2 | Email | Enter your registered email. | Must be a valid email format. |
| 3 | Continue | Sends the reset link to your email. | Check your inbox after clicking. |

## Common tasks

- **Request reset link** — Enter your email and click **Continue**. Check your inbox for the link.
- **Return to login** — Click **Back** to go back to the login page.

## Troubleshooting

- **No email received** — Check spam or junk. Use the resend option on the next screen if available.
- **Invalid email** — Enter the email you use to log in. The format must be valid (e.g. name@company.com).
- **Continue does nothing** — Check your connection and try again. Contact your admin if the problem continues.
