# Payrun Detail

## Purpose

View a single payrun: payment period, payslip status, total company spend, and the list of employee gross pay. From here you can edit the payrun, view history, or regenerate it.

## Screenshot

![Payrun Detail](/manual/pages/payroll-detail.png)

## On this page

1. **Edit Payruns** — Open the edit form for this payrun.
2. **Payruns History** — View history for this payrun.
3. **Regenerate Payrun** — Regenerate the payrun.
4. **Payment Period** — Shows the payrun period.
5. **Payslip Status** — Shows draft or final status.
6. **Total Company Spend** — Summary of total spend for this payrun.
7. **Employee Gross Pay List** — Section with employee count.
8. **Search by Employee Name, ID, or Department** — Filter the employee list.
9. **Employee payslips table** — Rows show Name, Working Hour, Allowance, Overtime, Additional Earnings, Penalty Deduction, Gross Pay, Nett Pay.
10. **Pagination** — Move between pages of employees.

## Common tasks

- **View payrun summary** — Check Payment Period, Payslip Status, and Total Company Spend.
- **Edit the payrun** — Click **Edit Payruns** to change details (URL includes the payrun ID).
- **Find an employee** — Use the search box to filter the Employee Gross Pay List.

## Troubleshooting

- **Empty spend or list** — Data may still be loading, or the payrun may have no payslips yet. If you expect data, ask your admin.
- **Cannot edit or regenerate** — These actions may be restricted by role. If you don't see the buttons, ask your admin.
