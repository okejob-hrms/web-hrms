# ESS Organization

## Purpose

This page shows your organization structure (org chart). You can see where you and your team sit in the company. Use the zoom controls to zoom in or out on the chart.

## Screenshot

![ESS Organization](/manual/pages/ess-organization.png)

## On this page

1. **Organization structure area** — Where the org chart is shown (tree or chart).
2. **Zoom 100%** — Shows current zoom level.
3. **Zoom out** — Decreases zoom (see more of the chart).
4. **Zoom in** — Increases zoom (see detail).

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Organization structure area | Displays the org chart. | May be empty if no data is set up. |
| 2 | Zoom 100% | Displays current zoom. | Read-only. |
| 3 | Zoom out | Zooms out. | See more of the structure. |
| 4 | Zoom in | Zooms in. | See names and roles clearly. |

## Common tasks

- **View the org chart** — Open the page; the chart loads in the main area.
- **Zoom in or out** — Use the minus and plus buttons to adjust zoom.
- **Find your position** — Look for your name or team in the chart (if shown).

## Troubleshooting

- **Chart area empty** — Organization data may not be set up yet. Ask your admin.
- **Zoom buttons not working** — Try refreshing the page. If it still fails, try another browser.
- **Can’t find my team** — The view may be scoped; use zoom out or ask your admin how the chart is configured.
