# Attendance List

## Purpose

View attendance summary and the list of attendance records. You can search by employee, add a new record, or open the row menu to view details, approve, reject, or edit a record.

## Screenshot

![Attendance List](/manual/pages/attendance-tracker.png)

## On this page

1. **Summary section** — Cards for Late Clock In, Early Clock In, Early Clock Out, On Time, Overtime, Absent, Day Off.
2. **Search by Employee Name or Email** — Type to find attendance records.
3. **New Record Attendance** — Open the Add Attendance form.
4. **Attendance table** — Rows show Name, Date, Check-In & Out, Shift, Notes, Status.
5. **Row actions** — Menu on each row (Attendance Details, Approve, Reject, Edit, Delete).
6. **Pagination** — Move between pages of results.

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |

## Common tasks

- **View summary** — Use the summary cards to see late, on time, absent, etc.
- **Add a record** — Click **+ New Record Attendance**, fill the form, and save.
- **Edit a record** — Open row actions and choose **Edit Attendance Record**. The URL includes employee ID and attendance ID (e.g. .../attendance-tracker/9/1).

## Troubleshooting

- **List is empty** — Check search; clear it or change filters. If you don't have access, ask your admin.
- **Cannot add or edit** — These may be restricted by role. If you don't see them, ask your admin.
