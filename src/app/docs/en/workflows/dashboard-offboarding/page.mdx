# Manage offboarding from dashboard

## When to use

Use this workflow when you want to see active or waiting offboarding items and open the offboarding list or a specific record.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin.
- You have permission to view offboarding.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard).
2. On the Pending Action Overview, click the card or link for Active Offboarding or Waiting Offboarding.
3. The dashboard shows the relevant list, or go to the full [Offboarding List](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding).
4. To work on a specific case, open an item to go to [Offboarding Detail](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding-detail). From there you can continue the offboarding steps or [process salary adjustment](/docs/workflows/salary-adjustment) if applicable.

## Expected result

You see active or waiting offboarding cases and can open the list or detail page to manage them.

## Common mistakes

- **No offboarding section** — Your role may not have access. Ask your admin if you need to manage offboarding.
- **Wrong status** — Use Active vs Waiting to filter the list you need.
