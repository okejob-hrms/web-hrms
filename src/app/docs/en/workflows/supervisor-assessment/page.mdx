# Complete supervisor assessment

## When to use

Use this workflow when you are a supervisor and need to complete an assessment for an employee (after they have submitted their self-assessment or when the period requires it).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as a supervisor or HR/Admin.
- You have permission to complete supervisor assessments.
- The employee has completed (or the period allows) the self-assessment, if that is required.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Supervisor Assessment List](/docs/manual/pages/supervisor-assessment) (under Performance).
2. Find the assessment you need to complete. Open it (e.g. from the row menu or by clicking the row). You are taken to the [Supervisor Assessment Detail](/docs/manual/pages/supervisor-assessment-detail) page (URL includes the assessment ID).
3. On the detail page, fill in the supervisor assessment form (questions, ratings, comments as per the template). Use the form template configured in [Supervisor Assessment Template](/docs/manual/pages/form-template-supervisor) (Settings).
4. Save or submit the assessment. The status updates and the employee (or HR) can see the result as per your company process.

## Expected result

The supervisor assessment is completed and submitted. It is recorded and can be used for performance review.

## Common mistakes

- **Cannot open assessment** — Your role may not have access, or the assessment may not be in a status that allows supervisor input. Ask your admin.
- **Missing questions** — The form is based on the template in Settings. If the form seems incomplete, ask your admin to check the [Supervisor Assessment Template](/docs/manual/pages/form-template-supervisor).
