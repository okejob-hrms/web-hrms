# View and act on pending items

## When to use

Use this workflow when you want to see and act on pending items (leave, overtime, attendance, offboarding, payslip requests) from the dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin.
- You have permission to view the dashboard and the relevant pending sections.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard).
2. Stay on the **Pending Action Overview** tab (default). You will see cards or links for pending counts (e.g. Active Offboarding, Leave Request, Overtime Request, Attendance Approval, Payslip Request).
3. Click the card or link for the type you want (e.g. Leave Request, Overtime Request). The dashboard content updates to show the list of pending items for that type.
4. Open an item from the list (e.g. a leave request or an attendance record) to view details, then use the actions on that screen to **Approve** or **Reject** (or follow the flow shown).

## Expected result

You see pending items and can approve or reject them. After you act, the item moves out of the pending list and the counts update.

## Common mistakes

- **No pending items shown** — There may be nothing pending, or your role may not have access. Check that you are on the correct tab and that the right overview is selected.
- **Cannot approve or reject** — The action may be on the detail screen (e.g. [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker) or leave list). Open the item and use the Approve/Reject options there. If you still do not see them, ask your admin.
