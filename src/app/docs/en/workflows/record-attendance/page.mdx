# Record attendance

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to record a new attendance entry (check-in/out or manual record) for an employee.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin (or as an employee if the app allows self-service attendance entry).
- You have permission to add attendance records.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker).
2. Click **New Record Attendance** (or **+ Add Attendance**).
3. On the [Add Attendance](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker-add) page, select the employee (if required), date, check-in/out times or other required fields. Fill any notes if needed.
4. Click **Save** (or **Submit**). Correct any validation errors if shown.
5. The new record appears in the [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker). It may need approval depending on your company setup.

## Expected result

A new attendance record is created and appears in the list. It may show as pending until approved.

## Common mistakes

- **Cannot see Add Attendance** — This may be restricted by role. Ask your admin.
- **Validation errors** — Ensure date and times are valid (e.g. check-out after check-in). Fill all required fields.
