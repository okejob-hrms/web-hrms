# Act on inbox item (supervisor)

## When to use

You are a supervisor and must approve or reject a request shown in your mobile **Inbox**.

## Prerequisites

- Supervisor account with inbox permissions.
- At least one pending item (leave, attendance, etc.).

## Steps

1. Sign in as supervisor and open the **Inbox** tab ([Mobile: Inbox](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-inbox)).
2. Tap a pending item.
3. Review details and choose approve or reject (wording may vary by request type).
4. Confirm if prompted.

## Expected result

The item leaves your pending list or shows as completed.

## Common mistakes

- **Empty inbox** — No pending approvals; verify seed data or ask HR to assign test items.
- **Wrong account** — Use a supervisor login, not a plain employee account.
