# Request a business trip

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to submit a business trip request as an employee (from ESS). HR/Admin use the separate [Business Trip](/docs/manual/pages/business-trip) list page to review submissions.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in with an ESS-enabled employee role.
- You know your trip **start and end dates**, **destination**, and **reason**.
- Destination and reason texts meet your company guidelines (attachments are not handled on this ESS form).

## Steps

1. Open [ESS](/docs/manual/pages/ess) and under **Quick Actions** choose **Business Trip**, or go directly to [ESS Business Trip](/docs/manual/pages/ess-business-trip).
2. Click **New Business Trip Request**.
3. In the modal, fill **Start Date** and **End Date** (\*each required). Confirm the end date is on or after the start date (the form blocks invalid ranges).
4. Enter **Destination** (e.g. city) (\*) and **Reason** describing the purpose (\*).
5. Click **Submit** when the Create button activates. Cancel discards unsaved edits.
6. Your new row appears under **Waiting** until an approver processes it ([Approve or reject a business trip](/docs/workflows/approve-business-trip)).

## Expected result

A business trip exists in **My Business Trip** with status **Waiting** (or progresses after HR action). Notifications depend on company setup.

## Common mistakes

- **Submit stays disabled / End date error** — Choose an end date on or after the start date and complete every starred field.
- **Modal closes without saving** — Use **Submit**, not navigation away, unless you deliberately cancel with **Cancel**.
- **No Quick Action tile** — If you don’t see **Business Trip** on ESS home, ask your admin.
