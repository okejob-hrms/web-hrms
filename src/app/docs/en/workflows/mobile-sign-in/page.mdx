# Sign in to the mobile app

## When to use

Use this workflow when you open **ESS CPS** on your phone and need to sign in with your work email and password.

## Prerequisites

- The mobile app is installed and can reach your company API.
- You have an employee account (email and password from HR).

## Steps

1. Open the ESS CPS app. If you are not signed in, you see [Mobile: Login](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-login).
2. Enter your **email** in the Email field.
3. Enter your **password** in the Password field.
4. Tap **Sign In**.
5. If this is your first login, complete [Mobile: Create Password](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-create-password) when prompted. Otherwise you land on [Mobile: Home](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-dashboard).

## Expected result

You are signed in and the **Home** tab shows your name, branch, and clock-in actions. The bottom navigation shows five tabs.

## Common mistakes

- **Email or password required** — Tap each field before typing; ensure both fields are filled.
- **Invalid credentials** — Confirm email and password with HR; use [Reset password on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-reset-password) if needed.
- **You opened Web ESS instead** — Use the installed mobile app for phone workflows; Web ESS is in the browser at `/ess`.
