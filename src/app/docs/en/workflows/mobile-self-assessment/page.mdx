# Complete self assessment on mobile

## When to use

Your company opened a self-assessment period and you must submit scores from your phone.

## Prerequisites

- Active assessment period configured in HRMS.

## Steps

1. Open **Performance** ([Mobile: Performance](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-performance)).
2. Tap **Self Assessment** ([Mobile: Self Assessment](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-self-assessment)).
3. Open an assessment row → [Mobile: Assessment Form](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-assessment-form).
4. Fill scores and comments, then submit.

## Expected result

Your self assessment shows as submitted for the period.

## Common mistakes

- **No assessments listed** — Period may not be open; ask HR.
- **Cannot submit** — Complete all required fields and sections.
