# Reset forgotten password

## When to use

Use this workflow when you forgot your password and need to set a new one via email.

## Prerequisites

- You have an HRMS account with a valid email address.
- You can access the email inbox for that address.

## Steps

1. On the [Login](/docs/manual/pages/login) page, click **Reset Password**.
2. On the [Reset Password](/docs/manual/pages/reset-password) page, enter your email and submit.
3. Check your email. You will receive a message with a link. See [Check Your Email](/docs/manual/pages/mail-confirm).
4. Open the link from the email. You are taken to the [Change Password](/docs/manual/pages/change-password) page.
5. Enter your new password and confirm if required, then submit.
6. You are taken to the [Password Changed](/docs/manual/pages/success-change-password) page. You can then sign in with your new password.

## Expected result

Your password is updated. You can sign in on the [Login](/docs/manual/pages/login) page with your email and the new password.

## Common mistakes

- **No email received** — Check spam; wait a few minutes. Use the email registered in the HRMS. If it still does not arrive, contact your admin.
- **Link expired** — Reset links are valid for a limited time. Request a new reset from the [Reset Password](/docs/manual/pages/reset-password) page.
