# View analytics

## When to use

Use this workflow when you want to view dashboard analytics (e.g. offboarding trend, charts by department, job level, or branch).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin.
- You have permission to view the dashboard.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard).
2. Switch to the **Analytics** tab (or the tab that shows charts and trends).
3. Use the charts and filters to view trends and breakdowns by department, job level, or branch as available.

## Expected result

You see the analytics view with the relevant charts and data.

## Common mistakes

- **Analytics tab empty or not visible** — Your role may not have access, or there may be no data yet. Ask your admin if you expect to see analytics.
- **Charts not loading** — Refresh the page; if it persists, check your connection and try again later.
