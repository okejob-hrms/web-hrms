# Edit company profile

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to change the company information or payroll bank details.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as Admin. You have permission to edit the company profile.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Company Profile](/docs/manual/pages/company-profile) page under Settings, Company.
2. Click Edit Company Information. You are on the [Edit Company Profile](/docs/manual/pages/company-profile-edit) page.
3. Change the fields as needed. Save.
4. You are returned to the Company Profile page. The updated information is displayed.

## Expected result

The company profile and payroll details are updated.

## Common mistakes

- **Cannot edit** — Your role may not have permission. Ask your admin.
- **Validation errors** — Ensure required fields are filled and formats are valid.
