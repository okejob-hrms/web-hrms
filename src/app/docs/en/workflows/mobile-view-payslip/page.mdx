# View payslip on mobile

## When to use

You want to see your payslip on your phone.

## Prerequisites

- Payslip data published for your employee record.

## Steps

1. Open the **Profile** tab ([Mobile: Profile](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-profile)).
2. Tap **My Payslip** ([Mobile: Payslip](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-payslip)).
3. Select a period. If your company requires approval, complete [Mobile: Payslip View Request](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-payslip-view-request).
4. Open [Mobile: Payslip View](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-payslip-view) to read details.

## Expected result

You can read payslip amounts for the selected period.

## Common mistakes

- **Empty payslip list** — HR may not have published pay data yet; ask your admin.
- **Print request** — Use [Mobile: Payslip Print Request](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-payslip-print-request) if you need a physical copy.
