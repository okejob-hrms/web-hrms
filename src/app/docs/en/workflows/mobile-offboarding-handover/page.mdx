# Complete offboarding handover on mobile

## When to use

You are in an active offboarding process and must complete handover steps on mobile.

## Prerequisites

- Offboarding record active for your account.

## Steps

1. Open offboarding from Home or ESS entry ([Mobile: Offboarding](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-offboarding)).
2. Complete [Mobile: Exit Form](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-exit-form) and schedule if needed ([Mobile: Exit Interview Schedule](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-exit-schedule)).
3. Submit [Mobile: Work Handover](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-work-handover), [Mobile: Document Handover](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-document-handover), and [Mobile: Responsibility Handover](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-responsibility-handover) as assigned.

## Expected result

Handover steps show completed in the offboarding checklist.

## Common mistakes

- **No offboarding card** — Offboarding may not be started; HR starts the process on web.
- **Cannot assign handover** — Verify you are the responsible employee for each step.
