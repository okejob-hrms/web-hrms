# Log in

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to sign in to the HRMS with your email and password.

## Prerequisites

- You have an HRMS account (email and password).
- You know the correct URL for the HRMS (e.g. provided by your company).

## Steps

1. Open the HRMS and go to the [Login](/docs/manual/pages/login) page.
2. Enter your **email** in the Email field.
3. Enter your **password** in the Password field.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. If this is your first login, you will be asked to [change your password](/docs/workflows/change-password) before continuing. Otherwise you are taken to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard) (HR/Admin) or [ESS](/docs/manual/pages/ess) (Employee).

## Expected result

You are signed in and see either the Dashboard or the ESS home, depending on your role. No error message is shown.

## Common mistakes

- **Wrong email or password** — Check spelling and caps lock. Use [Reset Password](/docs/manual/pages/reset-password) if you forgot your password.
- **"Signing in..." never finishes** — Check your internet connection; try again or contact your administrator.
