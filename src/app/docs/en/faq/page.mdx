# FAQ

Common questions and answers, grouped by topic. For step-by-step tasks, see [Workflows](/docs/workflows).

---

## Login

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
Use **Reset Password** on the [Login](/docs/manual/pages/login) page. Enter your email and follow the link sent to you. Then set a new password. See the [Reset forgotten password](/docs/workflows/reset-password) workflow.

**The system is asking me to change my password. Is that normal?**  
Yes. On first login (or when your admin has set a temporary password), you must set a new password before you can continue. Use the [Change password (first login)](/docs/workflows/change-password) workflow.

**I get "Invalid email or password" but I am sure they are correct.**  
Check that the email is the one registered in the HRMS and that caps lock is off. If you still cannot sign in, use [Reset Password](/docs/manual/pages/reset-password). If the problem continues, contact your administrator.

## Dashboard

**Where do I see pending leave and overtime requests?**  
Go to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard) and open the **Pending Action Overview** tab. Click the card for Leave Request or Overtime Request to see the list. See [View and act on pending items](/docs/workflows/dashboard-pending).

**I do not see the Analytics tab on the dashboard.**  
Analytics may be restricted by role or may require data to be present. If you expect to see it, ask your admin. See [View analytics](/docs/workflows/dashboard-analytics).

**How do I act on offboarding from the dashboard?**  
Use the Active Offboarding or Waiting Offboarding card on the Pending Action Overview, or go to the [Offboarding List](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding). See [Manage offboarding from dashboard](/docs/workflows/dashboard-offboarding).

## Employee

**How do I add a new employee?**  
Go to the [Employee List](/docs/manual/pages/employee-management) and click **Add Employee**. Fill in the required fields on the [Add Employee](/docs/manual/pages/employee-management-add) page and save. See [Add employee](/docs/workflows/add-employee).

**How do I edit an employee's details?**  
Open the employee from the [Employee List](/docs/manual/pages/employee-management) or their [Employee Detail](/docs/manual/pages/employee-management-detail) page, then choose **Edit**. See [Edit employee](/docs/workflows/edit-employee).

**I do not see the Add Employee button.**  
Adding employees is usually restricted to HR or Admin. If you need to add employees, ask your admin.

## Attendance

**How do I record attendance?**  
Go to the [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker) and click **New Record Attendance**. On the [Add Attendance](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker-add) page, fill in the employee, date, and times, then save. See [Record attendance](/docs/workflows/record-attendance).

**How do I approve or reject attendance?**  
From the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard) Pending Action Overview, open Attendance Approval, or go to the [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker) and filter by Pending. Open the record and use **Approve** or **Reject**. See [Approve or reject attendance or leave](/docs/workflows/approve-attendance).

**How do I request leave?**  
Employees: use [ESS](/docs/manual/pages/ess) and the leave section. HR/Admin: go to the [Leave Request List](/docs/manual/pages/leave-request) and click the button to add a request. See [Request leave](/docs/workflows/request-leave).

## Payroll

**Where do I see the list of payruns?**  
Go to the [Payrun List](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-list). From there you can open a payrun to see [Payrun Detail](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-detail) or [Edit Payrun](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-edit). See [View payrun list and details](/docs/workflows/view-payrun).

**How do I process a payrun?**  
Open the payrun from the list or detail page, then go to the [Edit Payrun](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-edit) page. Follow the steps (e.g. set gross pay, review). Complete or finalize as the app instructs. See [Process payrun](/docs/workflows/process-payrun).

**How do I request access to view or print my payslip?**  
Go to the [Request Access](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-request) page under Payroll and follow the on-screen steps. See [Request payroll access](/docs/workflows/payroll-access). On mobile ESS, after HR approves your view request, access is valid for **3 days**; request again when it expires. See [View payslip on mobile](/docs/workflows/mobile-view-payslip).

## Performance

**How do I complete my self-assessment?**  
Go to the [Self Assessment List](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment), open the relevant period or assessment form, and fill it in. You may use [Add Self Assessment](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment-add) or [Edit Self Assessment](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment-edit) depending on your role. See [Complete self assessment](/docs/workflows/self-assessment).

**How do I complete a supervisor assessment?**  
Go to the [Supervisor Assessment List](/docs/manual/pages/supervisor-assessment), open the assessment, and fill in the form on the [Supervisor Assessment Detail](/docs/manual/pages/supervisor-assessment-detail) page. See [Complete supervisor assessment](/docs/workflows/supervisor-assessment).

**How do I create or update an OKR?**  
Go to the [OKR List](/docs/manual/pages/okr). Click to add a new OKR or open an existing one to go to [OKR Detail](/docs/manual/pages/okr-detail) and edit. See [Create or update OKR](/docs/workflows/okr).

## Settings

**How do I add or edit user access?**  
Go to the [Access Control List](/docs/manual/pages/access-control) under Settings. Use **Add Access** for a new assignment or open an existing one for [Access Detail](/docs/manual/pages/access-control-detail). See [Add or edit user access](/docs/workflows/access-control).

**How do I change the company profile?**  
Go to the [Company Profile](/docs/manual/pages/company-profile) and click **Edit Company Information**. On the [Edit Company Profile](/docs/manual/pages/company-profile-edit) page, change the fields and save. See [Edit company profile](/docs/workflows/company-profile).

**How do I add a new leave type?**  
Go to the [Leave Configuration](/docs/manual/pages/leave-management) under Settings, use the Leave Type tab, and click **New Leave Type**. Fill in the form on the [Add Leave Type](/docs/manual/pages/leave-management-add) page and save. See [Configure leave types](/docs/workflows/leave-config).

**I do not see a Settings menu.**  
Settings is usually available only to Admin or HR. If you need to change settings, ask your admin.

## ESS

**What is ESS?**  
ESS (Employee Self-Service) is the area where employees view their own info, request leave, see attendance, complete assessments, and request overtime. See [Core Concepts](/docs/manual/core-concepts) and [Use ESS as employee](/docs/workflows/ess-home).

**I am an employee but I see the Dashboard instead of ESS.**  
Your account may have an HR or Admin role. If you should be an employee only, ask your admin to check your role and access.

**Where do I request leave as an employee?**  
Go to [ESS](/docs/manual/pages/ess) and open the Leave card or go to [ESS Leave](/docs/manual/pages/ess-leave). From there you can start a new leave request. See [Request leave](/docs/workflows/request-leave).

## Mobile app (ESS CPS)

**What is the difference between Web ESS and the mobile app?**  
Web ESS runs in your browser at `/ess`. **ESS CPS** is the Android/iOS app with five bottom tabs (Home, Performance, Leave Request, Inbox, Profile). See [Mobile ESS — Start Here](/docs/en/manual/mobile/start-here) and [Mobile Core Concepts](/docs/en/manual/mobile/core-concepts).

**How do I sign in on my phone?**  
Open the app, enter email and password on [Mobile: Login](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-login), and tap **Sign In**. See [Sign in to the mobile app](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-sign-in).

**I forgot my mobile password.**  
On the login screen tap **Reset Password** and follow the email link. See [Reset password on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-reset-password).

**Location or face verification failed when I clock in.**  
Allow location permission for ESS CPS. Stand within your work site radius. If face match fails, re-register your face or contact HR. See [Clock in on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-clock-in).

**Why is my Inbox empty on mobile?**  
Inbox items appear when you have approvals as a supervisor or when requests are assigned to you. Employees may see an empty inbox. Use the correct account or ask HR to assign a test approval.

**I do not see payslip or OKR on mobile.**  
Your company must publish payslip periods and OKR data. Open **Profile → My Payslip** or **Performance**. If still empty, ask your admin.

**Can I use the mobile app without GPS or face checks?**  
Production requires real location and face when your company enables them. Developers may use a staging bypass flag only during manual capture — end users always follow on-screen verification.
