# Terminology for Core Concepts (source for page-reference-writer)

## Glossary

- **Approval** – A request (e.g. leave, overtime, attendance) that a manager or admin must approve or reject before it is final.
- **ESS** – Employee Self-Service. The area of the app where employees view their own info, request leave, see attendance, and complete assessments.
- **Leave balance** – The number of leave days you have left for a leave type in the current period.
- **Offboarding** – The process when an employee leaves the company (resignation, end of contract, etc.). It can include handover, final pay, and deactivating access.
- **OKR** – Objectives and Key Results. Goals (objectives) with measurable results used to track performance.
- **Payrun** – A payroll run: the process of calculating and paying salaries for a group of employees for a given period (e.g. monthly).
- **KPI** – Key Performance Indicator. A measurable value used to see how well someone or a team is meeting a goal.

## Roles

- **Admin** – Full access. Can manage users, company settings, and all data.
- **HR** – Can manage employees, attendance, payroll, performance, and most settings. May approve leave and overtime.
- **Employee** – Uses ESS only. Can view own info, request leave and overtime, and complete self-assessments.

## Statuses

- **Pending** – Waiting for approval or action.
- **Approved** – Approved by a manager or admin; the request or change is accepted.
- **Rejected** – Not approved; the request is declined.
