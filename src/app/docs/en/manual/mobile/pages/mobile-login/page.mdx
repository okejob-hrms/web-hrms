# Mobile: Login

## Purpose

Use the **Login** screen in the ESS CPS mobile app to sign in with your work email and password. After signing in, you land on the **Home** tab. This is not the Web HRMS login in a browser.

## Screenshot

![Mobile: Login](/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-login.png)

## On this page

1. **Logo** — Application branding at the top of the form.
2. **Email** — Field for your work email address.
3. **Password** — Field for your password.
4. **Show/hide password** — Toggle to show or hide what you typed.
5. **Reset Password** — Opens the mobile reset-password flow.
6. **Sign In** — Submits your credentials and opens the app.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Logo | Shows application branding. | Display only. |
| 2 | Email | Enter your registered work email. | Must be valid email format. |
| 3 | Password | Enter your password. | Minimum length enforced by server. |
| 4 | Show/hide password | Toggles password visibility. | Use to verify typing. |
| 5 | Reset Password | Navigates to reset-password screen. | Use if you forgot your password. |
| 6 | Sign In | Signs you in and opens **Home**. | Disabled while request is in progress. |

## Common tasks

- **Sign in** — Enter email and password, then tap **Sign In**. You are taken to the [Mobile: Home](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-dashboard) tab.
- **Reset password** — Tap **Reset Password**, enter your email, and follow instructions in your inbox.
- **First login** — You may be asked to [create a new password](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-create-password) before using the app.

## Troubleshooting

- **Invalid email or password** — Check caps lock and that you use the email registered in HRMS. Try **Reset Password** or contact your admin.
- **Sign In does nothing** — Check mobile data or Wi‑Fi. Ensure the app can reach your company API.
- **Password field required** — Enter password again before tapping **Sign In**.
- **You expected Web ESS** — Install and use the mobile app for phone workflows; open `/ess` in a browser for Web ESS.
