# Mobile: Attendance & Overtime

## Purpose

**Attendance and Overtime** is your history hub: monthly attendance records, summary stats, and a separate **Overtime** tab for overtime requests. Open it from **Profile** to review past clock-ins or request a correction.

## Screenshot

![Mobile: Attendance & Overtime](/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-attendance.png)

## On this page

1. **Back** — Return to [Mobile: Profile](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-profile).
2. **Attendance tab** — List of daily attendance records (selected by default).
3. **Overtime tab** — List of overtime requests for the same month.
4. **Month selector** — Change the month shown in stats and lists.
5. **Summary stats** — Late clock-in days, early clock-out days, overtime hours, absent days, and day-off quota.
6. **Attendance heading** — Section title above the record list.
7. **Filter** — Filter attendance rows by status (All, Waiting, Approved, Rejected).
8. **Attendance record row** — Date, clock-in/out times, duration, status chip, and edit icon.
9. **Edit icon** — Opens [Mobile: Edit Attendance](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-attendance-edit) for that day.

## Common tasks

- **Review this month’s attendance** — Open **Profile** → **Attendance and Overtime**, confirm the month, scroll the list.
- **Request a time correction** — Tap the **edit** icon on a row → adjust times on [Mobile: Edit Attendance](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-attendance-edit).
- **Filter by approval status** — Tap **Filter**, choose Waiting, Approved, or Rejected.

## Troubleshooting

- **No attendance data for this period** — You may not have clocked in that month, or HR has not published records yet.
- **Cannot edit a row** — Some statuses may be locked after approval; ask HR.
- **Screen not available** — Ask your admin if you do not see **Attendance and Overtime** on Profile.

**Related workflow:** After you [Clock in on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-clock-in), new records appear here for the current month.
