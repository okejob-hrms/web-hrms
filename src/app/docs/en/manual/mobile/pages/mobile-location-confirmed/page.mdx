# Mobile: Location Confirmed

## Purpose

After the app verifies your GPS position, **Location Confirmed** tells you that you are within the allowed work area and prompts you to continue with face verification before clocking in or out.

## Screenshot

![Mobile: Location Confirmed](/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-location-confirmed.png)

## On this page

1. **Back** — Return to Home (cancels the current clock-in or clock-out attempt).
2. **Location Confirmed** — Success message that your position was accepted.
3. **Biometric illustration** — Visual cue that the next step is face verification.
4. **Instruction text** — Explains that face verification is required for secure attendance.
5. **Start Verification** — Opens [Mobile: Face Verification](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-face-verification) when you already have a registered face profile (three photos on file).
6. **Register Your Face** — Opens [Mobile: Face Registration](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-face-registration) when you have fewer than three face photos registered.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Back | Returns to [Mobile: Home](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-dashboard). | Cancels the in-progress clock action. |
| 2 | Location Confirmed | Status heading. | Shown after GPS validation succeeds. |
| 3 | Biometric illustration | Decorative icon. | Display only. |
| 4 | Instruction text | Describes the face step. | Same screen for clock-in and clock-out. |
| 5 | Start Verification | Continues to face verification. | Shown when your face profile is complete (3 photos). |
| 6 | Register Your Face | Continues to face registration. | Shown when you still need to register your face. |

## Common tasks

- **Continue after clock-in location check** — Tap **Start Verification** or **Register Your Face**, then complete the face step and [Mobile: Attendance Form](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-attendance-form).
- **Cancel** — Tap **Back** to return to Home without saving attendance.

## Troubleshooting

- **You never reach this screen** — Allow location permission for ESS CPS; stand inside your company’s allowed radius; wait for the “Verification in Progress” dialog to finish. If you see a distance error, move closer to the work site.
- **Wrong button label** — **Register Your Face** means you need to complete registration first; **Start Verification** means your profile is ready.
- **Screen not available** — Your company may disable mobile attendance. Ask your admin.

**Before you start:** You arrive here automatically after tapping **Clock In** or **Clock Out** on Home and passing location verification.
