# Mobile: Home (Dashboard)

## Purpose

The **Home** tab is your mobile dashboard after sign-in: company context, quick **Clock In** / **Clock Out**, shortcuts to overtime and leave, and recent activity. This replaces the Web ESS home in the browser — same employee tasks, different layout.

## Screenshot

![Mobile: Home (Dashboard)](/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-dashboard.png)

## On this page

1. **Company / branch** — Your assigned branch label.
2. **Employee name** — Your display name.
3. **Job title** — Your position.
4. **Today's date** — Current date on Home.
5. **Clock In** — Start today's attendance (location and face verification may apply).
6. **Clock Out** — End today's shift after you have clocked in.
7. **Overtime Request** — Shortcut to request overtime.
8. **New Leave Request** — Shortcut to create a leave request.
9. **Recent Activity** — Recent attendance or leave events (may be empty).
10. **Home tab** — You are on Home (selected in bottom navigation).
11. **Performance tab** — Open performance and OKR area.
12. **Leave Request tab** — Open your leave list.
13. **Inbox tab** — Open items waiting for your action.
14. **Profile tab** — Open profile and sign-out.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Company / branch | Shows branch name. | Display only. |
| 2 | Employee name | Shows your name. | Display only. |
| 3 | Job title | Shows position title. | Display only. |
| 4 | Today's date | Shows current date. | Display only. |
| 5 | Clock In | Starts clock-in flow. | May require GPS and face match. |
| 6 | Clock Out | Ends shift. | Disabled until you have clocked in. |
| 7 | Overtime Request | Opens overtime request. | Same as Performance/Leave paths. |
| 8 | New Leave Request | Opens new leave form. | See [Mobile: New Leave Request](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-leave-request-form). |
| 9 | Recent Activity | Lists recent events. | Empty until you have activity. |
| 10–14 | Bottom tabs | Switch main sections. | Five tabs: Home, Performance, Leave Request, Inbox, Profile. |

## Common tasks

- **Clock in** — Follow [Clock in on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-clock-in): **Clock In** → location → face (if required) → attendance form.
- **Clock out** — Follow [Clock out on mobile](/docs/en/workflows/mobile-clock-out) after you have clocked in.
- **Request leave quickly** — Tap **New Leave Request**.
- **Request overtime** — Tap **Overtime Request**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Clock Out is disabled** — You must **Clock In** first for the day.
- **Location or face failed** — Allow location permission; stand in range of your work site; re-register face if mismatch persists. Ask HR if problems continue.
- **No recent activity** — Normal for new accounts until you clock in or submit leave.
- **Missing a Web ESS feature** — Some admin features exist only in the browser; ask your admin.
