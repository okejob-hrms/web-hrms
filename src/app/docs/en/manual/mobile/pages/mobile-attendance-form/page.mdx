# Mobile: Attendance Form

## Purpose

The **Attendance Form** is the final step of clock-in or clock-out. Review your times and location, choose your shift (clock-in only), add notes, and submit to save today’s attendance.

## Screenshot

![Mobile: Attendance Form](/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-attendance-form.png)

## On this page

1. **Back** — Return to Home without submitting (discards this attempt).
2. **Attendance summary card** — Date, clock-in time, clock-out time, duration, overtime, and verified location.
3. **Shift** — Required dropdown to pick your working shift (clock-in only).
4. **Notes** — Optional text area for comments.
5. **Save Clock In** — Submits clock-in and returns to Home.
6. **Save Clock Out** — Submits clock-out and returns to Home.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Back | Returns to [Mobile: Home](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-dashboard). | Does not save attendance. |
| 2 | Attendance summary card | Shows date, times, duration, overtime, and GPS address. | Clock-out time may show “-” until you clock out. |
| 3 | Shift | Opens a list of shifts for the day. | Required on clock-in; read-only on clock-out. |
| 4 | Notes | Free-text notes sent with the record. | Optional. |
| 5 | Save Clock In | Saves today’s clock-in. | Footer label on clock-in flow. |
| 6 | Save Clock Out | Saves today’s clock-out. | Footer label on clock-out flow. |

## Common tasks

- **Finish clock-in** — Select a **Shift**, add **Notes** if needed, tap **Save Clock In**. You return to Home and **Clock Out** becomes available.
- **Finish clock-out** — Review times and location, add **Notes** if needed, tap **Save Clock Out**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Please fill all required fields** — Choose a **Shift** on clock-in; ensure location verification completed.
- **No active attendance found** (clock-out) — You must clock in successfully today before clocking out.
- **Error toast on save** — Check network connection; ask HR if your shift is not listed.
- **Returned to Home without saving** — Tap **Back** cancels; use the save button to record attendance.

**Before you start:** Complete [Mobile: Location Confirmed](/docs/en/manual/mobile/pages/mobile-location-confirmed) and face verification (if required).
