# Offboarding Detail

> **Flagged:** This section is draft documentation. The offboarding feature is not fully fixed yet. Screenshots and steps will be updated once the feature is complete.

## Purpose

View the details of a single offboarding record. The page shows the employee and offboarding information for the chosen record. The URL contains the record ID (e.g. .../off-boarding/16).

## Screenshot

![Offboarding Detail](/manual/pages/offboarding-detail.png)

## On this page

1. **Breadcrumb** — Shows Off Boarding and the record ID; use it to see where you are.
2. **Back to list** — Click "Off Boarding" in the breadcrumb to return to the list.
3. **Detail content or message** — Main area shows the offboarding details, or a message if the record is not found.

## Common tasks

- **Open detail** — From the offboarding list, click **Preview** on a row. The URL will be `.../off-boarding/{id}`.
- **Go to salary adjustment** — From this page you may have a link or action to open the salary adjustment page for this record (`.../off-boarding/{id}/salary-adjustment`).
- **Return to list** — Click **Off Boarding** in the breadcrumb.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Employee data not found"** — The record may have been removed or the ID may be invalid. Return to the list and open another record, or ask your admin.
- **Cannot see detail** — You may not have permission for this record. If you don't see it, ask your admin.
