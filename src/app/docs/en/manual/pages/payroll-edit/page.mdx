# Edit Payrun

## Purpose

Edit a payrun: set or review gross pay for each employee, then move to the next step to review the payrun. You can regenerate the payrun when reference data (attendance, leave, overtime, salary) has changed.

## Screenshot

![Edit Payrun](/manual/pages/payroll-edit.png)

## On this page

1. **Payruns History** — View history for this payrun.
2. **Regenerate Payrun** — Recalculate payroll from reference data (resets manual edits).
3. **Payment Period** — Shows the payrun period.
4. **Payslip Status** — Shows draft or final status.
5. **Completion steps** — Steps: 1 Gross Pay, 2 Review Payruns.
6. **Set Gross Pay** — Section with instructions; check pay rates and salary alignment.
7. **Employee Gross Pay List** — Heading and employee count.
8. **Search by Employee Name, ID, or Department** — Filter the employee list.
9. **Employee gross pay table** — Rows show Name, Working Hour, Allowance, Overtime, Additional Earnings, Penalty Deduction, Gross Pay, Nett Pay.
10. **Pagination** — Move between pages of employees.
11. **Cancel** — Go back without saving.
12. **Next** — Go to the next step (Review Payruns).

## Common tasks

- **Set gross pay** — Check the table, adjust if needed, then click **Next** to continue.
- **Regenerate** — Use **Regenerate Payrun** only when reference data changed; it recalculates and resets manual values.
- **Cancel** — Click **Cancel** to return without saving.

## Troubleshooting

- **Table is empty** — Data may still be loading, or the payrun may have no employees. Regenerate if you expect data. If you don't have access, ask your admin.
- **Cannot edit or regenerate** — These may be restricted by role. If you don't see the buttons, ask your admin.
