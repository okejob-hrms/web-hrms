# ESS Team Roster

## Purpose

Roster owners use this page in **Web ESS** to plan and publish the weekly roster for their teams. Team leads open the same page to see the published week. They cannot edit it.

Access comes from the job levels set in **Settings → Roster Setup**, not from being someone's primary supervisor on its own. Open it from the **Team Roster** tile in ESS quick access, or go to `/ess/team-roster`.

## What you can and cannot change

- A roster owner sets one shift for each team for the week, can change one member for the whole week, and can change one member for a single day.
- Publishing makes that week the roster employees clock in against. An ended week is locked; ask HR to change it.
- A team lead sees only the published week, read-only.
- There is no monthly grid and no bulk assign on this page.

## On this page

1. **Week** — Move to another week. Owners start on next week. Team leads start on the current week.
2. **Team shift** — The shift the whole team works that week (Morning, Evening or Night).
3. **Member override** — Change one person for the whole week, or for one day, including a day off.
4. **Publish** — Owners only. Confirms and publishes the week.

## Common tasks

- **Plan next week** — Set each team's shift, adjust any member who should differ, then click **Publish**.
- **Check your shift** — As a team lead, open the current week. You see the published roster only.

## Troubleshooting

- **Team Roster tile is missing** — Your job level is not a roster owner or team lead for this company, or you have no roster team. Ask HR to check **Roster Setup** and the reporting line.
- **"This week has not been published."** — The owner has not published it yet. Team leads cannot open a draft.
- **A past week will not save** — Ended weeks are locked in ESS. HR changes them under **Settings → Shift Roster**.
