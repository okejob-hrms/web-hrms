# ESS Team Roster

## Purpose

Supervisors use this page in **Web ESS** to plan shifts for their direct reports. You can set a shift or a day off for one day, fill a date range for several people at once, and create shift patterns (for example "2 days morning, 1 day off") and assign them to your team.

The page is only available if you are the **primary supervisor** of at least one employee. Open it from the **Team Roster** tile in ESS quick access, or go to `/ess/team-roster`.

## What you can and cannot change

- You can change **today and future dates** only. Past days are locked; ask HR to correct them.
- You can only change employees who report **directly** to you (primary reporting line).
- You can edit and delete **patterns you created** (owner **Mine**). Patterns created by HR (owner **HR**) can be assigned to your team, but not edited.
- If HR or another supervisor changes the same day, the **last saved change wins**. Hover over a cell to see who changed it last.

## On this page

1. **Roster tab** — Monthly grid: one row per team member, one column per day.
2. **Month** — Switch to another month, including future months.
3. **Roster cell** — Shows the shift name or **Off**. **M** means the day was set manually; **P** means it comes from a pattern. Locked (past) cells are greyed out.
4. **Bulk assign** — Set the same shift or day off for several team members over a date range.
5. **Patterns tab** — List of patterns you can use, with **Owner** (Mine or HR) and **Cycle** length.
6. **Create pattern** — Create a new repeating pattern.
7. **Actions** menu — **Assignees**, **Assign to team**, and (for your own patterns) **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | Roster tab | Shows the monthly team grid. | Default tab. |
| 2 | Month | Changes the month shown. | Future months are allowed for planning. |
| 3 | Roster cell | Click to open the edit dialog for that employee and day. | Only today and later can be edited. |
| 4 | Bulk assign | Opens a dialog to fill a date range for selected employees. | **On conflict** chooses **Skip existing manual** or **Overwrite**. Maximum range is 62 days. |
| 5 | Patterns tab | Lists your patterns and HR patterns available to your team. | Owner column shows **Mine** or **HR**. |
| 6 | Create pattern | Opens the pattern editor. | Set a name, cycle length, and a shift or day off for each day of the cycle. |
| 7 | Actions | Opens pattern actions. | **Edit** and **Delete** appear only for patterns you own. |

## Common tasks

- **Change one day** — On the **Roster** tab, click the cell for the employee and date, choose **Set shift**, **Set day off**, or **Clear manual**, then click **Save**.
- **Plan a week for several people** — Click **Bulk assign**, select employees, pick the date range and shift (or day off), then save.
- **Set up a rotating schedule** — On **Patterns**, click **Create pattern**, set the cycle, then use **Actions → Assign to team**. Pick employees, the **Anchor date** (day 1 of the cycle), and the **Effective from** date (today or later).
- **Stop a pattern for someone** — Use **Actions → Assignees**, choose an end date, and click **End**. Future pattern days after that date are removed.

## Troubleshooting

- **Team Roster tile is missing** — You are not recorded as the primary supervisor of anyone. Ask HR to check your reporting lines.
- **"Supervisors can only change today or future dates."** — You picked a past date. Choose today or later, or ask HR.
- **An employee is missing from the grid** — Only active employees who report directly to you are shown.
- **Edit or Delete is missing for a pattern** — The pattern was created by HR. You can still assign it.
- **A cell changed without you editing it** — Someone else (HR or another supervisor) saved it later. Hover over the cell to see who.
