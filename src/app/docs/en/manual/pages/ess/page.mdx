# ESS (Employee Self-Service)

## Purpose

ESS is the employee home screen. It shows quick links to request leave, request overtime, view your OKR, do self-assessment, and see your organization structure. It also shows your attendance trend and items waiting for your approval or action.

## Screenshot

![ESS](/manual/pages/ess.png)

## On this page

1. **Quick Actions** — Section with shortcut buttons.
2. **Leave Request** — Opens leave request (or the leave section).
3. **Overtime Request** — Opens overtime request (or the overtime section).
4. **My OKR** — Opens your OKR view.
5. **Self Assessment** — Opens self-assessment.
6. **Organization Structure** — Opens the organization chart view.
7. **Attendance Trend** — Card with your attendance trend (may include date range).
8. **Waiting For Approval** — Card showing items waiting for your action.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Quick Actions | Groups the main shortcuts. | Use the buttons below. |
| 2 | Leave Request | Goes to leave requests. | Request or view leave. |
| 3 | Overtime Request | Goes to overtime requests. | Request or view overtime. |
| 4 | My OKR | Goes to your OKR. | View or manage OKRs. |
| 5 | Self Assessment | Goes to self-assessment. | Start or continue assessments. |
| 6 | Organization Structure | Goes to org chart. | View where you sit in the org. |
| 7 | Attendance Trend | Shows your attendance over time. | May have date filters. |
| 8 | Waiting For Approval | Shows items pending your action. | Open an item to act on it. |

## Common tasks

- **Request leave** — Click **Leave Request** in Quick Actions, then create a new request.
- **Request overtime** — Click **Overtime Request**, then create a new request.
- **View or update OKR** — Click **My OKR**.
- **Do self-assessment** — Click **Self Assessment**.
- **See org structure** — Click **Organization Structure**.
- **Check what’s waiting** — Look at the **Waiting For Approval** card and open items as needed.

## Troubleshooting

- **ESS menu or page not visible** — Your role may not have ESS access. If you don’t see ESS, ask your admin.
- **Quick Action does nothing** — Make sure you’re logged in and try again. If it still fails, use the main menu or ask your admin.
- **Attendance or approval card empty** — There may be no data for the current period; try changing date range if available.
