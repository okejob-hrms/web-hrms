# Employee Detail

## Purpose

Use the Employee Detail page to view one employee’s full record. The URL contains the employee ID (e.g. `/employee/employee-management/12`). You can switch between Personal Information, Document, Payroll, Attendance, and Penalty, and open Edit from here.

![Employee Detail](/manual/pages/employee-management-detail.png)

## On this page

1. **Employee name and ID** — Shows the employee’s name and Employee ID at the top.
2. **Edit Employee Data** — Opens the Edit Employee page for this employee.
3. **Tabs** — Personal Information, Document, Payroll, Attendance, Penalty. Click to switch content.
4. **Personal Information content** — Face Recognition, User Role, Email, Phone, Gender, Place of Birth, Born Date, Marital Status, Blood Type, Height, Weight, ID Number, NPWP, BPJS, addresses, Hobby, Achievement, Social Media.
5. **Employee Information** — Position, Department, Job Level, Primary Direct Report, Additional Direct Report, Team, Employee Start Date, Employee End Date.
6. **Salary and Bank Information** — Base Salary, Salary (Nett), Bank, Account Number, Account Name.
7. **Family Information table** — List of family members; **Add Family Information** to add one.
8. **Formal Education** — **Add Formal Education** to add a record.
9. **Work Experience, Non Formal Education, Contact Reference** — Similar sections with Add buttons.

## Common tasks

- **View an employee** — Open from the Employee List (click row or row menu). The detail URL includes the employee ID.
- **Edit this employee** — Click **Edit Employee Data**, change fields, then **Update** on the edit page.
- **Add family or education** — In the relevant section, click **Add Family Information**, **Add Formal Education**, and so on.

## Troubleshooting

- **Page not found** — The ID in the URL may be wrong or the employee may have been removed. Go back to the Employee List and open the record again.
- **Edit Employee Data not visible** — You may not have permission. If you don’t see it, ask your admin.
- **Tabs show no data** — Some tabs load data when selected. Wait a moment or refresh.
