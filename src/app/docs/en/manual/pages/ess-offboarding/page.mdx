# ESS Offboarding

## Purpose

This page is shown when you are in an offboarding process. It tells you to complete your offboarding tasks so that your exit is smooth. Use it as the starting point for any forms or checklists your company requires.

## Screenshot

![ESS Offboarding](/manual/pages/ess-offboarding.png)

## On this page

1. **Offboarding tasks message** — Card that says “Complete Your Offboarding Tasks” and explains that completing tasks ensures a smooth exit.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Offboarding tasks message | Explains that you need to complete offboarding tasks. | Scroll down for task list or links if present. |

## Common tasks

- **Complete offboarding tasks** — Read the message and scroll or follow links to any forms or checklists (e.g. handover, exit survey).
- **Return to this page** — Use the ESS menu and choose Offboarding to come back.

## Troubleshooting

- **No task list or links** — Your company may add tasks over time. If you don’t see any tasks, contact HR or your manager.
- **Page not visible** — This page is usually only for employees in an active offboarding process. If you don’t see it, ask HR.
