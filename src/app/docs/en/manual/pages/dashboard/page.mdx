# Dashboard

## Purpose

The Dashboard is the main screen after you sign in. It gives you a single place to see what needs your action (leave, overtime, offboarding, payslips) and to view analytics, offboarding trends, payroll summaries, and assessment charts. Use the tabs at the top to switch between five views; within some views, use the **expand** icon on widgets to open a larger, detailed view.

## On this page

1. **Pending Action Overview (tab)** — Switches to the pending-action view (default tab).
2. **Analytics Dashboard (tab)** — Switches to analytics with charts and filters.
3. **Offboarding (tab)** — Switches to offboarding charts and list.
4. **Payroll (tab)** — Switches to payroll cards and Sum of Payroll chart.
5. **Assessment Dashboard (tab)** — Switches to assessment widgets and add-widget card.
6. **Summary cards (Pending Action)** — Show counts: offboarding, leave, overtime, payslip. No expand; use sidebar to drill down.
7. **Pending Action sidebar** — Lists Offboarding, Attendance Approval, Leave, Overtime, Payslip. **Drill-down:** click an item to show its list in the main area.
8. **Main content (Pending Action)** — Shows the list for the sidebar item selected. Open items from here to act.
9. **Expand icon (Analytics widgets)** — Small maximize icon on some widgets. Opens a **modal** with a larger or more detailed chart.
10. **Filters (Analytics / Offboarding / Payroll)** — Date, branch, department, etc. Change to refine the data shown.
11. **Eye icon (Offboarding table)** — In each row of the offboarding table. Opens the detail view for that record.
12. **Add Custom Chart Widget (Assessment)** — Card with plus icon. Opens a dialog to create a new chart widget.
13. **Expanded chart modal (Analytics)** — After clicking expand on a widget, a modal opens with a larger chart and extra breakdown (e.g. attendance trend with summary and employee table). Use **Close** or **Cancel** to dismiss.

### Tab 1: Pending Action Overview

![Pending Action Overview](/manual/pages/dashboard-pending.png)

- **Summary cards (top row)** — Five cards showing: Active Offboarding, Pending Leave Approvals, Employees On Leave Today, Pending Overtime Approval, Pending Payslip Request Approval. Each shows a count.
- **Sidebar (Pending Action)** — Use this to **drill down**. The sidebar lists: Offboarding (with sub-items Active Offboarding, Waiting of Offboarding), Attandance Aprroval, Leave Request, Overtime Request, Payslip Request. Click one to show the corresponding list in the main area.
- **Main content area** — Shows the list for the sidebar item you chose (e.g. list of leave requests, offboarding records, or payslip requests). From here you can open or act on each item.

There is no expand icon on the summary cards; the drill-down is done by choosing an item in the **Pending Action** sidebar.

### Tab 2: Analytics Dashboard

![Analytics Dashboard](/manual/pages/dashboard-analytics.png)

- **Filters** — Date period (start/end date), Branch, Department. Apply these to narrow the data in the charts.
- **Widgets** — Each widget is a card with a title and a chart (or list). Several widgets have a small **expand** (maximize) icon in the top-right corner. Clicking it opens a **modal** with a larger or more detailed view of that chart.
  - **Attendance Trend** — Line chart (on time, late, absent, overtime, leave). Has expand → opens detailed attendance modal.
  - **Total Employees** — Pie chart by category; total in the center.
  - **Experience Level** — Pie (e.g. Fresh Graduate vs Experienced). Has expand → experience modal.
  - **Age Spread** — Bar chart by generation. Has expand → age modal.
  - **Gender Spread** — Pie chart.
  - **Position** — Top positions with counts. Has expand → modal (Position/Job position breakdown).
  - **Teams** — Top teams with counts. Has expand → same style modal for Teams.
  - **Job Level** — Top job levels. Has expand → modal for Job level.
  - **Departments** — Top departments. Has expand → modal for Departments.

Use the expand icon when you want to see more detail or a bigger chart without changing filters.

#### Expand icon (Analytics)

Clicking the **expand** icon on a widget (e.g. Attendance Trend) opens a **modal** with a larger chart, summary breakdown, and sometimes a table (e.g. per-employee attendance). Close the modal with **Close** or **Cancel** to return to the dashboard.

### Tab 3: Offboarding

![Offboarding](/manual/pages/dashboard-offboarding.png)

- **Charts** — Offboarding trend (line chart), and stacked bar charts by department, job level, and branch (depending on data).
- **Filters** — Use to filter the data and the list.
- **Table** — List of offboarding records with columns such as Name, Position, Job Level, Department, Joined, Last Working Date. Each row has an **eye** icon; click it to **view that record** in detail.

### Tab 4: Payroll

![Payroll](/manual/pages/dashboard-payroll.png)

- **Summary cards** — Cards for Base Salary, Allowance, Overtime, Salary Deduction (Employee/Employer), Penalties, Payslip Request. Values may show percentage change. These cards do not have an expand icon; they are summary only.
- **Sum of Payroll** — A section with date range filters and a bar chart showing Overtime, Allowance, and Total Salary by month.

---

### Tab 5: Assessment Dashboard

![Assessment Dashboard](/manual/pages/dashboard-assessment.png)

- **Widgets** — One or more chart or table widgets (configured for your org). Each shows a label and either a bar chart or a table of labels and values.
- **Add Custom Chart Widget** — A card with a plus icon and the text “Add Custom Chart Widget.” Click it to open a dialog where you can create a new chart widget and add it to this dashboard.

## Common tasks

- **See what needs your action** — Open the Dashboard; stay on Pending Action Overview. Use the **Pending Action** sidebar to pick “Leave Request,” “Overtime Request,” “Active Offboarding,” etc., and the main area shows that list.
- **Drill down from summary counts** — Use the sidebar under Pending Action (e.g. “Leave Request”) to see the list; the summary cards only show counts.
- **View a bigger or detailed chart (Analytics)** — Find the widget (e.g. Attendance Trend, Experience Level, Age Spread, Position, Teams, Job Level, Departments) and click its **expand** icon to open the detailed modal.
- **Review offboarding** — Open the Offboarding tab; use the table and click the **eye** icon on a row to view that record.
- **Check payroll summary** — Open the Payroll tab; review the summary cards and the “Sum of Payroll” chart. Set the date range if needed.
- **Add an assessment widget** — Open the Assessment Dashboard tab and click **Add Custom Chart Widget** to create a new chart widget.

## Troubleshooting

- **Tabs not showing** — You may not have access to all tabs. If you don’t see a tab, ask your admin.
- **No data in a list** — In Pending Action, make sure you selected the right item in the sidebar. Else check filters or date range.
- **Expand icon does nothing** — Ensure you’re clicking the maximize icon on the widget (Analytics tab). If it still doesn’t open, try refreshing; if you don’t see the icon, that widget may not support expand.
- **Counts don’t match the list** — Counts are live; refresh the page if you think something is missing.
- **Add Custom Chart Widget not visible** — If you don’t see it on the Assessment Dashboard, ask your admin about permissions.
