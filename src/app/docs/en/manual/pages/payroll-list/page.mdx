# Payrun List

## Purpose

View and manage payruns. You can search by name, filter by date, create a new payrun, or open a row to see payrun details and edit it.

## Screenshot

![Payrun List](/manual/pages/payroll-list.png)

## On this page

1. **Search by payruns name** — Type to find payruns.
2. **Pick a date** — Filter payruns by date.
3. **New Payruns** — Open the form to create a new payrun.
4. **Payruns table** — Rows show Payruns (payroll month with its date range, for example *September 2026* and *27 Aug 2026 – 26 Sep 2026*), Total Payslip, Total Pay, Payslip Status, Generation Status.
5. **Row menu** — Open actions for a payrun (e.g. view detail, edit).
6. **Pagination** — Move between pages of results.

## Common tasks

- **View payruns** — Use the table to see periods, totals, and status. Click a payrun name to open details.
- **Create a payrun** — Click **+ New Payruns**, check the **Start date** and **Cutoff date**, and save. See [Create a payrun](/docs/workflows/create-payrun).
- **Filter by date** — Use **Pick a date** to narrow the list.
- **Regenerate a failed payrun** — The **Regenerate** button appears only for draft payruns whose generation failed.

## New payrun form

| Field | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| Start date | First day of the pay period. | Filled in automatically: the day after the previous payrun ended. |
| Cutoff date | Last day of the pay period. | Filled in automatically, keeping the same cutoff day as earlier payruns (a 28th or 30th cutoff is shortened in February and returns in March). |
| Notes | Free text for HR. | |

The payroll month (the name of the payrun) is the month with the most days in the period: 27 Aug – 26 Sep is the *September* payrun, and 27 Sep – 26 Oct is the *October* payrun. If both months have the same number of days, the cutoff month is used. A pay period can be at most 31 days, and each payroll month can have only one payrun.

## Troubleshooting

- **List is empty** — Check search and date filter; clear them if needed. If you don't have access, ask your admin.
- **Cannot create payruns** — This may be restricted by role. If you don't see **+ New Payruns**, ask your admin.
- **"The pay period overlaps an existing payrun"** — Another payrun already covers some of these dates. Change the start date, or cancel the other payrun first.
- **"Some days are not covered"** — There is a gap between this payrun and the previous or next one. Attendance and overtime on those days will not be paid. Adjust the dates, or choose **Create anyway** if the gap is intended.
- **"Pay period has not ended"** — The cutoff date is today or later. Payslips created now miss attendance, overtime and penalties after today. Wait until the period ends, or choose **Generate anyway**.
- **Regenerate is missing** — Finalized, sent and cancelled payruns cannot be regenerated.
