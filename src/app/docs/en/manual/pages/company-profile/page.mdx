# Company Profile

## Purpose

View the company information and payroll (bank) details. Use **Edit Company Information** to change them.

## Screenshot

![Company Profile](/manual/pages/company-profile.png)

## On this page

1. **Company Information** — Logo, name, Legal Entity, Industry, Email, Phone, Business Registration Number, Website, Address.
2. **Payroll Information** — Bank Account Name, Number, Holder, Currency.
3. **Edit Company Information** — Open the edit form.

## Controls

| Number | Name | What it does | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Company Information | Displays company details. | |
| 2 | Payroll Information | Displays bank details. | |
| 3 | Edit Company Information | Goes to edit form. | If you don't see this, ask your admin. |

## Common tasks

- **View profile** — Check company and payroll sections.
- **Update details** — Click **Edit Company Information**, change fields, then save.

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot edit** — You may need permission. If you don't see the button, ask your admin.
