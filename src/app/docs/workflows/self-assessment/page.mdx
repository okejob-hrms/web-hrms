# Complete self assessment

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to create or complete a self-assessment period or fill in your own assessment form.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in. You have permission to add or edit self-assessments.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Self Assessment List](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment).
2. To create a new period: Click Add Self Assessment to open [Add Self Assessment](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment-add). Set the period and participants, then save.
3. To complete your assessment: Open the relevant period. Use [Edit Self Assessment](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment-edit) or [Assessment by Period/Employee](/docs/manual/pages/self-assessment-period). Fill in the form and submit.
4. After submission, the assessment status updates.

## Expected result

A new assessment period is created or your self-assessment is submitted. It appears in the list with the updated status.

## Common mistakes

- **Cannot add period** — Your role may not have permission. Ask your admin.
- **Form does not submit** — Ensure all required questions are answered. Check validation messages.
