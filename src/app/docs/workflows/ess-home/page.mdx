# Use ESS as employee

## When to use

Use this workflow when you are an employee and want to use the Employee Self-Service (ESS) area to view your info, request leave, check attendance, complete assessments, and more.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in with an employee account (or a role that sees ESS after login).
- Your admin has given you access to ESS.

## Steps

1. After [log in](/docs/manual/pages/login), you are taken to the [ESS](/docs/manual/pages/ess) home (if your role is employee). If you see the Dashboard instead, you are likely HR/Admin; ESS is for employees.
2. On the ESS home you see cards or links for: Leave, Organization, OKR, Assessment, Overtime, Offboarding (if applicable). Click the card or link for the task you want.
3. **Leave** — [ESS Leave](/docs/manual/pages/ess-leave): view leave balance, submit a [leave request](/docs/workflows/request-leave).
4. **Organization** — [ESS Organization](/docs/manual/pages/ess-organization): view org chart.
5. **OKR** — [ESS OKR](/docs/manual/pages/ess-okr): view or create OKRs.
6. **Assessment** — [ESS Assessment](/docs/manual/pages/ess-assessment): view assessment periods and [complete self assessment](/docs/workflows/self-assessment).
7. **Overtime** — [ESS Overtime](/docs/manual/pages/ess-overtime): view or [request overtime](/docs/workflows/request-overtime).
8. **Offboarding** — [ESS Offboarding](/docs/manual/pages/ess-offboarding): view your offboarding tasks if you are leaving.

## Expected result

You can complete common employee tasks from ESS: request leave, view org and OKR, complete assessments, request overtime, and see offboarding tasks when relevant.

## Common mistakes

- **I do not see ESS** — Your account may have an HR/Admin role that sees the Dashboard instead. If you are an employee and still do not see ESS, ask your admin.
- **A card or link is missing** — Some features depend on your role or company setup. If you expect to see a feature, ask your admin.
