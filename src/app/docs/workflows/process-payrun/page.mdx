# Process payrun

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to process a payrun (e.g. set gross pay, review payruns, complete the payrun steps).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin with payroll permission.
- You have a payrun to process (from the [Payrun List](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-list) or [Payrun Detail](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-detail)).

## Steps

1. Go to the [Payrun List](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-list) and open the payrun you want to process, or open it from [Payrun Detail](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-detail).
2. Go to the [Edit Payrun](/docs/manual/pages/payroll-edit) page (e.g. from the row menu **Edit** or the button on the detail page). The URL includes the payrun ID.
3. Follow the completion steps shown (e.g. **1. Gross Pay**, **2. Review Payruns**). In the Set Gross Pay section, review or adjust employee gross pay as needed. Use the employee table to see Working Hour, Allowance, Overtime, Deductions, Gross Pay, Nett Pay.
4. When all steps are done, use the button to complete or finalize the payrun (e.g. **Regenerate Payrun** if you made changes, then complete). Save or submit as the app instructs.

## Expected result

The payrun is processed and reaches the expected status (e.g. completed). Payslips can be generated or requested as per your company process.

## Common mistakes

- **Cannot edit payrun** — The payrun may already be completed or locked. Your role may not have permission. Ask your admin.
- **Gross pay or totals wrong** — Check that attendance, allowance, and deduction settings are correct. Use the employee table on the Edit Payrun page to verify figures before completing.
