# Process salary adjustment

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to process or adjust salary for an offboarding employee (final pay, prorated amount).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin.
- You have permission to process offboarding salary adjustments.
- An offboarding case exists. Open it from [Offboarding Detail](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding-detail).

## Steps

1. Open the offboarding case from the [Offboarding List](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding) or [Offboarding Detail](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding-detail).
2. Go to the salary adjustment step, or open [Offboarding Salary Adjustment](/docs/manual/pages/offboarding-salary-adjustment). The URL includes the offboarding ID.
3. Review or enter the salary adjustment details. Follow the on-screen steps.
4. Save or submit the adjustment. Confirm if the app asks for confirmation.

## Expected result

The salary adjustment for the offboarding employee is recorded and can be used for final pay processing.

## Common mistakes

- **Page not found** — Open the adjustment from the offboarding detail or list so the URL has a valid offboarding ID.
- **Cannot save** — Check required fields and validation messages. If you do not have permission, ask your admin.
