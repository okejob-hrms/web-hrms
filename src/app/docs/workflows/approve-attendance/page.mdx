# Approve or reject attendance or leave

## When to use

Use this workflow when you are a manager or admin and need to approve or reject pending attendance records or leave requests.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in as HR or Admin (or as a manager with approval rights).
- There are pending attendance or leave items assigned to you or visible to you.

## Steps

1. Go to the [Dashboard](/docs/manual/pages/dashboard) and open the **Pending Action Overview** tab. Click the card for **Leave Request**, **Attendance Approval**, or **Overtime Request** as needed.
2. Alternatively, go directly to the [Leave Request List](/docs/manual/pages/leave-request) or [Attendance List](/docs/manual/pages/attendance-tracker) and filter by Pending (or similar).
3. Open the item you want to act on (e.g. a leave request or an attendance record). Use the **Approve** or **Reject** button (or the row menu **Approve** / **Reject** on the list).
4. If rejecting, enter or select a reason if the app requires it. Confirm. The item status updates and disappears from the pending list.

## Expected result

The leave request or attendance record is approved or rejected. The requester can see the updated status. Pending counts on the dashboard update.

## Common mistakes

- **No pending items** — There may be nothing pending for you, or you may be looking at the wrong tab/filter. Check the dashboard pending section and the correct list.
- **Approve/Reject not visible** — Your role may not have approval permission for that type. If you expect to approve these items, ask your admin.
